#4471 split · three problems, not one

owner support desk · draft · reviewed 2026-11-04

#4471 was merged as one known issue. It holds three different problems. The merge followed decision 0001: same subject line, same ticket. Nobody read the bodies, and the cause of a report does not depend on its subject.

Not one issue, one fix. Fixing the parser closes most of #4471, not all of it.

  1. Parser reads dates month first. Tenants write the day first (every tenant on file does), and the shared parser flips day and month. Reported by 11 tenants, from the app, email and the form on booking-v2.
  2. booking-v3 form hint. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so people at day-first tenants type month first. A different bug with a different fix (the hint, not the parser). It only happens through the form, at the three tenants on booking-v3: alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour.
  3. One day off. The reports say "Tuesday vs Monday", "only June", "Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday", "the invite arrived before I booked". The month is right and the day is off by one. That points at time zones or clock handling, not date parsing. The cause is not confirmed.

Intake tags are not the cause

Intake tags each report from the reporter's browser, as a guess. The tag and the real cause (from tenant and form, see causes.ts) disagree often: 23 parser reports are tagged hint and 15 hint reports are tagged parser. Count by cause, not by tag.
